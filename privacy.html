Children's information

Children never sign up to Familate themselves, and we never ask a child for an email address or any contact detail. A grown-up aged 18 or over, who confirms they are the child's parent or guardian, creates the family and adds each child.

  • A child appears in the app under whatever name the grown-up types. A first name or nickname is all it needs.
  • A child's own phone joins the family with a one-time code shown by a grown-up. That phone shows only that child's chores, stars and rewards, and a grown-up can disconnect it at any time.
  • A child can mark a chore as done, choose a reward to save for and claim a reward. Children cannot message anyone, cannot see other families, and nothing they do is visible outside their own family.
  • Young people aged 16 or 17 can be added as helpers ("grown-ups" in the app). We never collect an email address for anyone under 18.
  • There are no adverts, no in-app purchases, no location tracking and no profiling.

Parents and guardians can see, change or delete everything stored about their child from inside the app, and can ask us to do it for them.

About a month before an unused family is deleted, we email its grown-ups so they can keep it by opening the app. If that email could not be sent, deletion waits until 13 months.

Deleted information is removed from our live systems straight away and drops out of our provider's routine backups shortly afterwards. Usage totals that identify no one are kept.

Your choices and rights

Most things you can do yourself in the app, at once and without asking us.

  • Change a name or colour: tap the person on the Today screen.
  • Remove a child, including their stars and history: tap the child, then "Remove from the family".
  • Disconnect a child's phone: tap the child, then "Own phone".
  • Delete your own account, or the whole family with everything in it: open "Family settings". Step by step.
  • Stop notifications: switch them off for Familate in the phone's settings.

Under data protection law you also have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Parents and guardians can use these rights on behalf of their children. Email welcome@familate.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Security and changes

Information travels between the app and our database over encrypted connections and is stored encrypted. Each family can reach only its own information, and a child's phone can reach only that child's. There are no passwords: grown-ups sign in with a one-time code sent by email.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever put your information at risk, we would tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

We will update this policy when the app changes what it stores or how. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell grown-ups in the app or by email before any change that matters takes effect.
