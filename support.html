Support

Stuck, or found something that does not work? Email welcome@familate.com and a real person will reply.

Signing in

Familate has no passwords. Type your email address and we send you a code. Type the code into the app and you are in. If the email does not arrive within a minute, check your spam folder, then ask for a new code.

Connecting a child's own phone

On a grown-up's phone, tap the child's name on the Today screen, then "Own phone". A code appears. On the child's phone, open Familate, tap "Joining a family with a code?" and scan or type it. Each code works once and lasts 15 minutes.

Adding another grown-up

On the Today screen, add a grown-up and enter their email address. They can then sign in on their own phone with that address. Grown-ups without an email address, and helpers aged 16 or 17, join with a code instead.

Deleting your account or your family

You can delete everything yourself, in the app, at any time.

  • To remove one child with their stars and history: tap the child on the Today screen, then "Remove from the family".
  • To delete only your own account: open "Family settings", then "Delete my account". The family stays for the other grown-ups. If you are the only grown-up with full access, delete the family instead.
  • To delete the whole family and every account in it: open "Family settings", then "Delete this family".

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you cannot get into the app, email welcome@familate.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it for you.

A family that nobody opens for 12 months is deleted automatically. We email its grown-ups about a month before.
