How it works

Done, checked, earned.

A paper star chart lives in the kitchen and only one person keeps it up. Familate is the same idea, shared by the whole family.

  1. Grown-ups set it up

    Add your children, give each of them chores worth 1 to 5 stars, and choose the rewards they can save for.

  2. Children do the chore

    They tap it as done. Every grown-up's phone buzzes, so someone can go and look at that bedroom.

  3. A grown-up approves

    Only then are the stars given. Not quite done yet? Send it back with one tap and they try again.

Built for real families

One phone or six. It works either way.

Share one phone
Children pick their own name on a grown-up's phone. Getting back to the grown-up view takes Face ID or a PIN.
Or give children their own
A child's phone joins with a code a grown-up shows. No email, no password, and it only ever shows that child's chores.
Saving for something
Each child picks one reward to save towards and watches the stars add up. Changing their mind is free.
More than two grown-ups
Grandparents, a childminder or a responsible older sibling can help approve chores.
Every chore is checked
Children are clever. Nothing earns stars until a grown-up has said yes.
Private by design
No adverts, no tracking, no location. Children never give an email address. Read the privacy policy.
